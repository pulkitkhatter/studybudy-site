FAQ

Good questions

How does Studybudy pick my buddy?

By the exam you are preparing for, your timeline and how you like to study. The more of your plan overlaps, the higher the match.

Do I have to talk on camera?

No. Focus rooms are built for quiet, side-by-side work. Chat is there when you want it.

Can I change my buddy?

Yes. If it isn't working, request a new match any time.
